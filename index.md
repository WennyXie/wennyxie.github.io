## About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Virginia, advised by <a href="https://shangtongzhang.github.io/">Prof. Shangtong Zhang</a> in the <a href="https://github.com/Sequential-Intelligence-Lab">Sequential Intelligence Lab (SIL)</a>.

My research studies how learning algorithms and LLM agents improve through experience. I began with reinforcement learning theory, studying convergence and finite-sample guarantees, and extended this work to understanding how Transformers implement reinforcement learning in context. I then explored how LLMs can contribute to formal mathematics through lemma generation (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02561">MathlibLemma</a>) and assessing contributions beyond formal correctness (<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07147">MathlibPR</a>). Most recently, during my internship at Microsoft Research Asia, I worked with <a href="https://chang-xu.github.io/">Dr. Chang Xu</a> on self-evolving agents that learn from past modifications to predict and guide future improvements (<a href="https://wennyxie.github.io/assets/files/IDM.pdf">IDM</a>).

I received my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Fudan University, where I worked with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DrcEuSkAAAAJ">Prof. Zhongzhi Zhang</a>. In 2023, I was an exchange student at the University of Alberta and joined <a href="https://xiaoqitan.org/" style="white-space: nowrap;">Prof. Xiaoqi Tan</a>'s Learning, Optimization, and Games reading group.

**I am seeking research internships for Summer 2027. Feel free to [reach out](mailto:xie.zixuan@email.virginia.edu)!**
